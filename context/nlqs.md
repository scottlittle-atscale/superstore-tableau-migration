# Natural-language queries the model must answer

## From the Tableau Superstore workbook (21 worksheets, 6 dashboards)

1. What were total sales and profit by product category and sub-category last year?
2. Which customer segment has the highest profit ratio?
3. Show sales by state and city, with the regional manager for each region.
4. What is profit per order, and sales per customer, by segment?
5. How many days does it take to ship on average, by ship mode?
6. Which orders were returned, and what share of sales did returns represent?
7. Show monthly sales trend with year-to-date and prior-year comparisons.
8. How did sales compare against the sales target by category and segment?
9. Which regions are above or below their sales target?
10. What is each salesperson's booked sales by region?
11. Rank customers by sales within each segment.
12. Show the top sub-categories by sales, filtered to profitable orders only.

## From the LOD pack (phase two — Tableau's 15 LOD workbooks + Flerlage's 20 use cases)

13. What is the average order value (mean of per-order sales totals)?
14. What was each customer's first order date, and how do cohorts compare?
15. What share of total sales does each category represent?
16. Which orders were profitable overall, and what share of sales came from them?
17. What is each customer's lifetime sales, bucketed into bands?
18. Compare each sub-category's sales to its parent category total.
19. How many distinct customers ordered in each month, and how does that compare to last year?
20. What is sales per customer at the order grain versus the line grain?
